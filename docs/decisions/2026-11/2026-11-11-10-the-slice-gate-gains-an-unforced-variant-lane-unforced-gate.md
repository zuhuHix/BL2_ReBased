## 2026-11-11: The slice gate gains an unforced variant (lane unforced-gate)

AI-assisted (Claude Opus 5.5). Test behaviour only; no gameplay rule changes. Record: `docs/verification/SLICE_GATE.md`, "The unforced variant".

- **Why.** The gate has passed 14 of 14 since 2026-11-07, but four links pass only on stand-ins: the forced gun, the granted Phaselock point, the lethal-damage down and the respawn after it. The mission is taken with the use key alone, after the range is cleared. So the gate showed that the host can do each step, not that the loop happens.
- **What changed.** `tools/test_slice_gate.ps1 -Unforced` (host switch `-owslicegateunforced`) runs the same 14 links without those stand-ins. It kills range enemies until a gun drops (100 kills or 720 s), skips the heal and revive, buys the point on the skills page, takes and turns in the mission with Enter on Marcus's mission page, leaves the range as it is, and has Maya stand by the enemies without firing for up to 90 s instead of the lethal damage. Where a link cannot get there unforced, the old stand-in still runs so the later links are tested, and the link names it as a fixture.
- **Grading.** The start line carries `unforced=1`. `tools/check_slice_gate.py` fails, in that variant only, any link that names a forcing fixture, and any PASS on links 5, 9, 10, 12, 13 or 14 without its `how=` token. With the range there, a NOT_AVAILABLE on the loot or the down link is a FAIL.
- **Older variants unchanged.** Every new branch in `OpenWillowSliceGate.cpp` is behind the switch, and the strings it adds are empty without it. The runner adds the switch and the page URLs only under `-Unforced`. The checker grades the older variants as before.
- **Choices (host, UNVERIFIED).** The kill budget comes from an estimated gun chance of 0.043 per kill (0.085 x the stage-8 weapon share 0.508): 100 kills miss about 1.2 % of the time. Maya fires and casts nothing during the down wait, and the limit is 90 s. Both are recorded in SLICE_GATE.md with their arithmetic.
- **Verified (automated):** `tests/check_slice_gate_test.py` 55 of 55 (16 new: older-variant fixtures and verdicts kept, every forcing fixture failed, the `how=` tokens, the NOT_AVAILABLE overrules, `--expect-unforced`, the runner's launch line). 400 random older-variant logs gave the same exit code, table, JSON and markdown from the checker on main and this one.
- **Not verified:** the C++ was not compiled (the build slot was held by a test batch) and no variant has run in the engine. Whether the skills page takes the Slate Enter, whether the mission page shows in the gate's session, how long the kills take, and whether enemies down Maya are all open until the integration runs. Nothing is checked against the real game.
