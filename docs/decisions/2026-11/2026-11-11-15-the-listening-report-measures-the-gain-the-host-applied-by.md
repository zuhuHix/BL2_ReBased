## 2026-11-11: The listening report measures the gain the host applied by fitting each played file; the play line names its file (audio mix lane, follow-up to PR #217)

AI-assisted (Claude Opus 5.5). Record: `docs/verification/SLICE_AUDIO_CHAIN.md` section 7a.

- **Finding.** In the first recording with the game's gains, every 2D sound reaches the mix at its map gain minus about 3 dB, mono and stereo alike (least-squares fit of the played file: equip and Phaselock 3.0 dB under their map gains, correlation 1.000; the gains themselves are in the ignored `local/slice/audio_play.json`). The 8 dB the RMS table showed came from its reference: the shot's window also holds its impact and a second shot 3 ms later.
- **Change.** `OpenWillowAudio` logs `file=<name>` with each play. `tools/listen_report.py` fits each cue's file in the recording (numpy) and bases "off by" on the applied gain minus the map gain, against the shot's. The lag is searched at full rate by FFT (review r1 on PR #221: a search on every 8th sample missed broadband sounds off a multiple of 8). Each cue is paired with the play line of its role just before it. A cue with another cue of its role within 0.4 s gets no fit, a role with no fit shows no "off by", and a fit on a guessed file is marked.
- **Verified.** Rerun with the full-rate fit on that recording: equip -0.2, Phaselock -0.2, clip in 0.0, clip out +0.4 dB off (unchanged; `fire` fitted on a guessed file, the recording predates `file=`). Python tests (synthetic): listen report 33/33 without numpy, listen report fit 10/10 with numpy (registered in the numpy group). The first equip of each recording, which did not fit, starts 2 to 8 ms before its cue: the search now starts 50 ms before each cue, and it fits at the same gain as the others. **UNVERIFIED:** where the common 3 dB comes from; why only the first equip comes early; the C++ change is uncompiled; nobody has listened.
