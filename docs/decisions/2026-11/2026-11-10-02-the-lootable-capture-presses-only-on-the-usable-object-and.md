## 2026-11-10: The lootable capture presses only on the usable object, and frames the opened strong box from its door side (vendors lane)

AI-assisted (Claude Opus 5.5). Capture behaviour change in `tools/real_game/loot_capture.ps1` and `scripts/loot_capture.py`; record in `docs/verification/VENDORS.md` section 9 ("Strong box, next run").

- **Press gate.** The use key is pressed only once the player's `CachedUsableObject` is the target, read up to 5 times per side with a nudge between reads (aim lower, step closer, re-aim). The reason: two real runs saw it set on all 5 tries that opened and empty on all 7 that did not. A side that never offers it is skipped unpressed, and a type that never offers it fails with a clear message. This applies to every lootable type.
- **Strong box.** Up to `-StrongBoxes` (3) placed boxes are tried in path order. Once one opens, the capture saves a frame from the press stand, with its real distance recorded in `current_from`. It then saves frames from the door side and the opposite side, each at 150 uu. The door side comes from the bone that turned most: the object's origin is turned about that bone by the same turn, and the floor direction it moves in is snapped to a quarter turn of the facing. If no bone turned, the facing stands in.
- **After run Z53 (hub's runner, at 502d12b).** The strong box opened, on side 2 after the step. Its door bone turned about 125 degrees without moving, so the first test (bone position) fell back to the facing. Run offline on that run's reads, the turn-based test gives the side the open door was seen from. The storage locker's `CachedUsableObject` stayed empty on all 20 reads, where run 2 read the locker itself from the same spot. A blind-press fallback for the locker was then considered. Empty reads now also record the object's state and the distance.
- **Run Z53b (at c06172d) and review r2.** Every lootable opened, the locker through the gate on its first read. `door_view.from` was `lid bone`, and the door frame shows the open box (read by the hub). The door test returns no side when the two bones that turned most are 1 degree apart or less, or when the origin moved 1 uu or less across the floor (reviews r2 and r3).
- **Blind fallback removed (hub decision after review r3).** Distance and aim do not prove the locker is the nearest usable object, and a neighbour could become the target between check and press. The fallback was not needed either: the locker opened through the gate on its first read in Z53b and Z53c. Every type is pressed only when the game offers it as the usable object.
- **Run Z53c (at f3bd081).** The dumpster was the usable object on side 4's first read, and then failed with a bare PowerShell null error before its closed frame. It was not an empty reply from the game. The line was not recorded, and the cause is not pinned (the focus check, the frame grab or the burst). The press steps now fail only their side, recording the error and its script line, and the capture tries the next side. An empty game reply is reported as such. Cash failed because an eridium stick matched the cash test (its definition sits under `gd_currency`); eridium is now its own kind.
- **Record.** The object's transform and its bones' world transforms (closed and opened) are saved. Nearby pickups now carry world positions and offsets in the object's frame, not only distances.
- **Unchanged:** pending record, backup and verified restore, `block_saves`, input release, locks. The cleanup tail is still word for word the reviewed one, and every new step runs after the backup and the save block.
- **Verified (automated):** `tests/real_game_loot_capture_test.py` and `tests/real_game_capture_safety_test.py`. New cases: no press without the usable object, moving on to another box, door frames only for the strong box, the turn-based door side with its tie (including exactly 1 degree) and small-move guards, no press on an empty field for any type, an empty reply or a failing press step failing only its side, and eridium not counting as cash.
- **Not verified:** what raised Z53c's null error. Why the locker's field was empty in Z53. The hinge has not been compared with the host's.
