## 2026-11-10: `AGENTS.md` is the single agent brief, `CLAUDE.md` imports it, and the status log leaves it

AI-assisted (Claude Fable 5), at the maintainer's request. Documentation only; no code, parser, dependency or licence change.

- **Why.** `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` repeated each other (priority, external extraction, sensitive files, tools), and `CLAUDE.md` told Claude to read `AGENTS.md` first, so a session took in about 30 KB of instructions. `AGENTS.md` also carried a "Where things stand" log last dated 2026-10-23. Assistants were told to read it first, and much of it was out of date.
- **What changed.** `AGENTS.md` holds every rule once, as eleven numbered rules followed by the working sections. `CLAUDE.md` imports it with an `@AGENTS.md` line and keeps only the guard hook and the sub-agent briefing notes. Together they are about 17 KB. The status log is replaced by pointers to `ROADMAP.md`, `docs/verification/GATES.md` and the verification records. `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `README.md` and `docs/LANE_BRIEF.md` describe the new arrangement.
- **Rules written into the brief that it did not state before.** Rule 10 (never kill a process by image name, never delete a lock you do not hold) and rule 11 (nothing is sold, the original game stays required; `docs/LEGAL.md`). A real-game session needs the maintainer's or the brief's explicit ask, as `docs/LANE_BRIEF.md` already said for lanes.
- **One reading rule narrowed.** The old text said to read `docs/LEGAL.md` and the README's "Is this legal?" section first, always. The brief now carries the short form and says to read the full text before work on native analysis, licences, provenance, reuse of third-party code, releases or anything published, money, or anything that moves game-derived data. `docs/LEGAL.md` still wins where it says more.
- **Checked.** The Claude CLI loads `AGENTS.md` through the import (a session quoted rule 10 without reading the file). Every path the two files name exists, and `python tools/decisions_index.py --check` passes. An independent review (Luna) compared the old and new texts rule by rule and against `docs/LEGAL.md`; its ten findings were applied. The status log's claims were found in `DECISIONS.md`, `ROADMAP.md` or the verification records before it was removed.
- **Not checked.** `ctest` and `tools/verify_packages.py` were not run: the change is Markdown only and no test reads these files. `tools/mission_closure.py` still cites `CLAUDE.md` in one message string for the UModel material note, which now lives in `AGENTS.md`.
